Our publishing standards

Useful advice. Clear limits. Better choices.

PhoneYa2 publishes phone-accessory buying guidance and practical device-care information for people shopping in Zambia. This page explains how we approach accuracy, product information, updates and commercial relationships.

Important: Our guides are general information, not a substitute for the instructions for your exact device or a qualified repairer's assessment. Product prices, stock and specifications can change; use the current listing and confirm uncertain details before ordering.

What we aim to publish

We aim to answer real customer questions in plain language: how to identify a connector, compare accessory types, check a case's model fit, troubleshoot common settings, and know when a device needs professional attention. Articles should help readers make a decision or complete a task, not merely repeat product keywords.

Accuracy and product claims

Device specifications and compatibility can vary by model and region. We encourage readers to check manufacturer documentation for model-specific requirements. We do not treat a product title, image or marketing claim as proof of a technical specification. If a PhoneYa2 listing does not make a key detail clear, contact us before purchasing.

We do not claim to have independently laboratory-tested a product unless that testing is explicitly described on the relevant page. General guides should not be read as a guarantee that every accessory works with every phone.

Authorship and review

Guides attributed to the PhoneYa2 Editorial Team are prepared for PhoneYa2 readers. We aim to review content for clarity, practical usefulness, safety and consistency with the information available. Where a device-specific answer depends on exact specifications, readers should verify it with the manufacturer or a qualified technician.

Updates and corrections

Product availability and prices may change more quickly than educational guidance. We update content when we identify an important error, when instructions become outdated, or when the reader experience needs improvement. If you find a mistake or unclear claim, please contact PhoneYa2 with the page link and the detail that needs attention.

Commercial relationships

PhoneYa2 is an online accessories business, so some pages link to products available in our catalogue. Product links are intended to help readers find relevant items; they do not replace independent consideration of compatibility, price and quality. If advertising appears on the site, it should be distinguishable from editorial content and must not be mistaken for a product recommendation.

Reader privacy and support

For information about data handling, read our Privacy Policy. For ordering, delivery or return questions, review our Return & Refund Policy and contact us if anything is unclear.
