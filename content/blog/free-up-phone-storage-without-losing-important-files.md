A phone case should fit your exact model—not just look similar in a photo. PhoneYa2's shop helps you browse available accessories and open a product listing before deciding what suits your phone.

## Start with your exact phone model

Check the model name in your phone's Settings, or look at the device information provided by its manufacturer. A case made for a different model may cover buttons, cameras or ports even when the phones look almost identical.

## Choose the kind of protection you need

- **Slim and clear:** useful when you want to keep the phone's original look while adding a basic layer of protection.
- **More coverage:** look for raised edges and reinforced corners if your priority is everyday knocks.
- **Grip and handling:** choose a finish that feels secure in your hand and is easy to keep clean.

No case makes a phone drop-proof. Keep the screen and camera area in mind when comparing designs.
