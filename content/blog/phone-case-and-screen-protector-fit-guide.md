## How to Choose a Phone Case and Screen Protector That Actually Fit

A case or screen protector can look right in a product photo and still be the wrong fit. Phone names often include several versions—standard, Plus, Pro, Pro Max, 4G and 5G—and small differences can change the camera opening, button position or screen dimensions.

Use this checklist before you order.

## 1. Confirm the exact model

Check Settings → About phone, the device's model information, or the manufacturer's documentation. Do not rely only on the name printed on the back of a box or what a seller calls it.

Pay attention to suffixes such as Plus, Pro and Pro Max, as well as the model generation. A case designed for one generation may not fit the next even if the phones look similar.

## 2. Compare the camera layout

Turn the phone over and compare the number, size and position of the camera openings with the case photo. Also check whether the flash, microphone and any additional sensors have their own openings.

A case with a camera cut-out in the wrong place may cover part of a lens or prevent the case from sitting properly. Do not assume that cases for two models are interchangeable because the camera islands look similar.

## 3. Check the edges, buttons and ports

A suitable case should sit securely without bending or leaving corners loose. Confirm that its cut-outs align with the charging port, speaker, microphone and buttons. If the case has covered buttons, they should still be usable without excessive force.

Think about how you use your phone. A slim case takes up less space, while a raised edge or reinforced corner may offer extra protection from everyday bumps. No case can guarantee that a phone will survive every drop.

## 4. Choose the screen protector type carefully

Tempered-glass protectors and flexible film protectors have different feel and installation characteristics. Before choosing, check the protector's stated model compatibility, whether it covers the flat viewing area or curves around the edges, and whether it is designed to work with your case.

A protector that extends too far toward the edge may lift when a tight-fitting case presses against it. A smaller protector may leave more of the screen uncovered. Read the dimensions and product description where available.

## 5. Install it with care

Before installation, wash and dry your hands. Work in a clean, well-lit place. Follow the protector's supplied instructions, clean the screen using the materials provided, and line up the speaker and camera openings before lowering it into place.

Avoid forcing glass into position or pressing hard on a cracked protector. If the protector breaks during installation, handle the fragments carefully and replace it. Do not use a protector with sharp edges.

## 6. Ask these questions before paying

- Is the listing for my exact phone model and variant?
- Do the camera, charging port and side buttons line up?
- Does the screen protector work with the case I intend to use?
- What is included in the package?
- Is the displayed image representative of the listed product?
- What are the seller's terms if the item is supplied incorrectly?

When shopping online, compare the listing title and description rather than selecting only by the image. If compatibility is not stated clearly, ask the seller to confirm it before paying.
