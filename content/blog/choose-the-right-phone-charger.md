A charger can fit your phone and still be the wrong choice. Check these essentials before paying.

## The 4-point charger check

- **Connector:** Confirm whether your phone uses USB-C, Lightning or Micro-USB. Do not guess from appearance alone.
- **Charging support:** Your phone must support the charger's charging standard to benefit from fast charging.
- **Power output:** More watts do not automatically mean faster charging. Compare the charger's specifications with your phone's requirements.
- **Safety and condition:** Choose a well-built adapter and cable. Avoid damaged insulation, loose parts, unusual heat, buzzing or a burnt smell.

## Do not forget the cable

The adapter and cable work together. Make sure the cable has the right connectors and supports the charging you need. Replace frayed or intermittently disconnecting cables.

## Before you order

1. Check your exact phone model and charging port.
2. Read the product title, specifications and current price.
3. If compatibility is unclear, ask the seller before ordering.
