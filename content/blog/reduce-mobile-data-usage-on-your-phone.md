PhoneYa2 brings product listings together in an online shop, so you can browse accessories before deciding what to order. Here is a straightforward way to use the site.

## Step 1: Open the shop

Go to the [PhoneYa2 shop](/shop.html). You can browse the catalogue or use the search field to look for a product type, such as a cable, case, charger or earphones.

## Step 2: Open the product listing

Do not choose based on the title alone. Open the item and review the available image, description, displayed price and any compatibility details. If the information does not answer your question, ask before ordering.

## Step 3: Check that it matches your device

Connectors and compatibility matter. Check whether your phone uses USB-C, Lightning or another connection. For a case, confirm the precise model. For wireless accessories, check the listed features and what your device supports.

## Step 4: Proceed carefully with your order

Follow the current order instructions shown by PhoneYa2. Review your selected product and contact details before submitting. Keep the order confirmation for reference. If a page or instruction looks unclear, pause and contact the shop instead of guessing.

## Step 5: Return to the catalogue when comparing

You can return to the [shop](/shop.html) to compare other listings. Check the live product page each time; prices and availability may change.

**Useful habit:** make your decision from the current product listing—not an old screenshot, a forwarded price or an assumption about what is included.
