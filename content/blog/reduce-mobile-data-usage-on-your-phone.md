If mobile data disappears faster than expected, a few settings can help you understand where it goes and avoid unnecessary background use.

## 1. Find the biggest data users

Open Settings and look for **Network & Internet**, **Connections** or **Mobile data usage**. Menu names vary by phone. Check which apps used the most data during your current billing period.

## 2. Stop unnecessary background data

For apps that do not need constant updates, restrict background data if your phone supports it. Keep essential messaging, work or security apps configured the way you need them so you do not miss important alerts.

## 3. Change video and social media settings

Video can use a lot of data, especially at high quality. Choose a lower playback quality when watching on mobile data, turn off autoplay where possible, and download large videos or music over trusted Wi-Fi when that is practical.

## 4. Control automatic downloads and updates

- Set app-store updates to Wi-Fi only if that suits your situation.
- Review automatic media downloads in messaging apps.
- Disable automatic backups over mobile data if your plan is limited.
- Check whether cloud photo syncing is using data in the background.

## 5. Use data-saving modes

Many Android phones and some apps have a Data Saver or Low Data Mode. Turn it on when you need to stretch a bundle, then review whether any app you rely on needs an exception.

## 6. Check hotspot use

If you share your connection with a laptop or another phone, set a hotspot password and turn the hotspot off when finished. Connected devices may download updates or stream video without you noticing.

## 7. Measure before buying another bundle

Check your provider’s official usage information and your phone’s data counter. They may calculate periods differently, so compare the same dates. If usage seems unusually high, check app-by-app usage and whether your hotspot or cloud backup was active.

**Try this today:** record your current data usage, turn off one unnecessary background activity, and check again after a day of normal use. Change one setting at a time so you can see what helps.
