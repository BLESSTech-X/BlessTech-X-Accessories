PhoneYa2 is not only a product catalogue. It also has an advertiser page for Zambian businesses that want another place online to present what they sell or the services they offer.

## What the advertiser page is for

The [PhoneYa2 advertiser page](/advertiser) is the place to start if you want to submit a business advertisement. The offer is designed to let businesses promote their products or services without an upfront advertising fee.

## Prepare your ad before submitting

For a clearer listing, prepare:

- Your business or brand name.
- A short, accurate description of the product or service.
- A clear image that you have permission to use.
- A working way for interested people to contact you.
- Correct prices or offer details, if you choose to include them.

Keep the wording specific. Explain what you offer, who it is for and what the customer should do next. Do not promise results you cannot guarantee.

## What to do next

1. Open the [PhoneYa2 advertiser page](/advertiser).
2. Read the current form instructions.
3. Submit accurate details and check them before sending.
4. Keep a copy of what you submitted so you can follow up if necessary.

Submitting an ad does not mean it is automatically approved or guaranteed to produce sales. Use the current page for the latest requirements and status.
