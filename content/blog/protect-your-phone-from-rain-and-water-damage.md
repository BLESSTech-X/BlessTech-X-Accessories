Rain, spills and damp pockets can damage a phone—even if it still seems to work immediately afterward. Water resistance varies by model, and seals can weaken as a device ages or gets repaired.

## If your phone gets wet

1. Move it away from the water and power it off if you can do so safely.
2. Disconnect the charger and accessories. Never handle a plugged-in device with wet hands or while standing in water.
3. Wipe the outside gently with a soft, dry cloth.
4. Remove the case and dry it separately.
5. Leave the phone in a dry, ventilated place and follow the manufacturer’s instructions before turning it on or charging it.

## Avoid these common mistakes

- **Do not charge a wet phone.** Moisture in a port can cause corrosion or electrical damage. If the phone reports moisture, follow its instructions and wait until the port is dry.
- Do not use a hair dryer, heater, oven or direct sunlight to force it dry.
- Do not put it in rice; dust and particles can enter ports, and rice is not a reliable repair method.
- Do not push tissue, cotton buds or metal objects deep into the charging port.
- Do not assume a water-resistance rating means the phone can be submerged or exposed to every kind of liquid.

## Prepare before the rain

- Use a suitable protective case and keep the phone in a secure, dry pocket or bag.
- Keep it away from open drink containers and wet surfaces.
- If you commute or work outdoors, use a sealed pouch designed for your situation—but check its condition and closure before relying on it.
- Keep backups of important photos, contacts and documents. Water damage can happen suddenly.

## When to get professional help

Seek qualified repair advice if the phone was submerged, will not start, becomes unusually hot, shows screen or camera moisture, or behaves strangely after getting wet. If the battery is swollen, smoking or hissing, keep away from it and seek urgent local assistance.

**The safest habit:** keep the phone dry, back up important information, and never connect power to a device or port that may still be wet.
