A product photo is only the starting point. Before ordering an accessory, check the listing details and make sure it matches what you actually need.

## Four checks to make on a product page

1. **Look closely at the photo.** Check the connector, shape and visible features. If a detail is not shown, do not assume it is included.
2. **Read the description.** Confirm the type of accessory, stated features and any compatibility information.
3. **Check the displayed price.** Make sure you understand the price shown on the listing before proceeding.
4. **Match it to your device.** For cables and chargers, check the connector and charging support your phone accepts. For cases, match the exact phone model.
