A power bank can be useful during travel, long days away from a socket, or when you need a backup charge. Before buying one, look beyond the large capacity number printed on the box.

## 1. Choose capacity around your routine

A higher mAh rating generally means more stored energy, but conversion losses mean the energy delivered to your phone is lower than the number on the label suggests. Think about whether you need a top-up during the day or several charges while away from power.

## 2. Check the output, not only the capacity

Charging speed depends on the power bank's output and the phone's supported charging standard. Check the listed output for each port and confirm compatibility with your device. A high-capacity power bank may still charge slowly if its output is limited.

## 3. Count the ports you will actually use

If you regularly charge a phone and another device, check whether the power bank can power both at once and whether the total output changes when multiple ports are in use.

## 4. Balance capacity with portability

Larger power banks are often heavier and take longer to recharge. Consider where you will carry it: a pocket, handbag, backpack, or travel kit. The best choice is one you are comfortable taking with you.

## 5. Inspect the condition and specifications

Look for clear capacity and output ratings, a sturdy casing, undamaged ports, and instructions from the manufacturer. Avoid units that are swollen, cracked, leaking, unusually hot, or have a chemical smell. Do not charge or use a damaged power bank.

## 6. Remember that the power bank needs recharging too

Check the input port and the time it may take to recharge the unit. If you need it every day, a power bank that takes too long to recharge may not fit your routine.

## Make the choice easier

Before paying, answer three questions:
- How many extra phone charges do I need in a typical day?
- What charging speed does my phone support?
- Will I carry this every day, or only on longer trips?

Compare the answers with the product specifications rather than choosing by capacity alone. If the listed details are unclear, ask the seller before buying.
