## What you need

For routine cleaning, use a clean, soft, lint-free cloth. For stubborn marks on the exterior, follow the phone or accessory maker’s instructions; some devices permit a small amount of suitable isopropyl-alcohol solution on the cloth, while others have different guidance. **Never spray liquid directly onto a phone, connector or accessory.**

## Clean the screen and phone body

1. Power the phone off and unplug all cables.
2. Remove the case.
3. Wipe the screen and exterior gently with a dry microfibre cloth.
4. If needed, slightly dampen the cloth—not the phone—and avoid openings, speaker grilles and ports.
5. Dry the surface completely before reconnecting power.

Avoid paper towels that can scratch some surfaces, household window cleaner, abrasive powders, bleach and spraying cleaners into the device. Do not assume a water-resistance rating means a phone is safe to wash or soak; seals can wear over time.

## Clean a phone case

Remove the case from the phone first. For silicone or plastic cases, use a soft cloth and mild soapy water only if the case manufacturer permits it. Rinse the case away from the phone, wipe off residue and let it dry fully before putting it back on. Leather and fabric cases may need specialised care, so check the maker’s instructions.

## What about the charging port?

A charging port is delicate. First switch off the phone and inspect it under good light. Do not pour liquid into the port, insert metal pins, scrape the contacts or force a tool inside. If lint is packed tightly, the connector is damaged, or charging only works at a particular angle, have a qualified repairer inspect it. Stop using the port if you notice corrosion or signs of liquid damage.

## Keep cables and earbuds clean too

- Disconnect accessories from power before cleaning.
- Wipe cable exteriors with a dry cloth; never immerse a cable or adapter.
- Do not bend a cable sharply at the connector or pull it out by the wire.
- Clean earbuds according to their manufacturer’s guidance. Keep moisture away from speaker openings and charging contacts.
- Replace accessories with exposed wires, cracked housings, loose plugs or signs of overheating rather than trying to repair them with tape.

## Make it a small routine

A quick, gentle wipe every few days can help keep fingerprints and everyday grime under control. Clean more often when a case or accessory gets visibly dirty, but avoid excessive moisture and aggressive chemicals. The safest cleaning method is the one recommended for your specific device.
