## First, understand what is normal

Phones can feel warm while charging, during video calls, navigation, gaming, hotspot use or large downloads. Warm does not automatically mean dangerous. **A phone that becomes too hot to handle comfortably, displays a temperature warning, smells unusual, swells or has a damaged battery needs immediate attention.**

## If your phone is getting unusually hot

1. **Stop heavy use.** Close games, video recording, navigation or other demanding tasks.
2. **Unplug it if it is very hot or showing a warning.** If safe to do so, disconnect the charger at the plug or power source; do not handle damaged cables or a smoking device.
3. **Move it to a cool, dry, shaded place.** Keep it away from sunlight and heat. Let it cool naturally.
4. **Remove the case if it can be removed safely.** This may help heat escape. Do not open the phone or remove a built-in battery.
5. **Check the charging setup once it has cooled.** Look for frayed cable insulation, bent connectors, a loose port, a damaged adapter or an incompatible charger. Do not keep testing equipment that appears damaged.
6. **Try a known-good compatible charger and cable.** Use products from a reputable source and follow the phone maker’s guidance. If overheating returns, stop charging and seek qualified help.

## Avoid these dangerous “quick fixes”

- Do not put the phone in a fridge or freezer; condensation can damage it.
- Do not pour water on it or use a wet cloth on charging equipment.
- Do not press, puncture or attempt to flatten a swollen battery.
- Do not continue charging a device that is smoking, hissing, leaking or visibly swollen. Keep away and contact local emergency services if there is a fire or immediate danger.

## Before buying a replacement charger

Check the connector your phone uses, the charging standard and the power rating supported by your model. A higher wattage printed on an adapter does not automatically make it suitable for every phone. A compatible phone and charger manage the power they use, but quality and condition still matter.

**Get help promptly** if the phone repeatedly overheats while idle or with a suitable charger, the charging port is damaged, or the battery looks swollen. A new cable is not a safe fix for a damaged battery.
