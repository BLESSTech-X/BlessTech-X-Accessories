What your package pays for

Every partner gets a business website, catalogue and publishing tools. Paid packages add higher priority in the shared campaign rotation, richer website styling and deeper reporting. Approved campaigns can appear on PhoneYa2 and participating partner websites. Rotation weight is a relative priority—not a promise of a fixed number of views, clicks or sales.

How payment works: choose a package → PhoneYa2 receives your request → our team calls you → you pay directly using the method we agree → an administrator confirms payment and activates your package. No online payment is collected here.
