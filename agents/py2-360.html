How the affiliate program works

A simple product-first system—no sales target calculator or measurement dashboard to manage.

01

Choose a product

Browse the live PhoneYa2 catalogue and open a product to understand its features, price, stock and estimated earning.

02

Share your product link

Use WhatsApp sharing or copy the product message and link. Add your issued affiliate code if PhoneYa2 has provided one.

03

Help the customer decide

Explain the product honestly, answer questions and confirm current availability. Never promise a sale or commission before confirmation.

04

Earn on eligible sales

Estimated earnings appear on every product. A sale must meet the active PhoneYa2 affiliate rules before commission is payable.

Important about earnings and tracking

Estimates use the fallback tiers currently built into this page because a verified active affiliate-rate feed is not connected here. Referral links include tracking parameters, but that alone does not prove a sale was attributed or guarantee a payout. PhoneYa2 should confirm eligible sales and the final commission.
