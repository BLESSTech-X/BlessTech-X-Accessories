Our Story

Built by BLESSTech-X, for Zambia

BLESSTech-X is a digital solutions company founded by Barack Blessings and Sylvester Mwila in Lusaka, Zambia. We build websites, e-commerce stores, and digital systems that help Zambian businesses grow.

PhoneYa2 Accessories is one of the ventures born from BLESSTech-X – a practical example of what we build for our clients. We saw a gap in the market for affordable, genuine phone accessories delivered fast in Lusaka, so we built PhoneYa2 to fill it.

Today, PhoneYa2 serves hundreds of customers across Lusaka with same-day delivery, genuine products, and personal service through WhatsApp. It's our proof that the digital solutions we build for others actually work.
