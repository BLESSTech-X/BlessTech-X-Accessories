Before you buy an accessory

  1. Confirm your exact phone make and model, not just the brand.
  2. Check connector type and any charging or compatibility requirements.
  3. Read the product description, current price, stock status and any stated limitations.
  4. Ask the seller before ordering if an important detail is unclear.
  5. Confirm delivery or collection arrangements and the payment process before you commit.

These guides provide general information, not a guarantee that a particular device or accessory is fault-free. For damaged batteries, electrical faults or persistent charging problems, stop using the affected equipment and seek qualified help.
